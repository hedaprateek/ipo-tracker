The short version

There is no account, no server, no database and no analytics. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Everything the app remembers is stored by your own browser and stays on the device you typed it on.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps a few things in your browser's local storage. This is not a cookie and is not transmitted with requests — it is a box on your device that only this site can read.

Stored asWhat it holds
ipotracker.ids The PANs or DP/Client IDs you add, with the labels you give them.
ipotracker.registrars Which registrar you last picked for each issue.
ipotracker.history, ipotracker.snapshot A local copy of grey market premium readings, so a chart still draws when a source is down.
ipotracker.filters, ipotracker.range, ipotracker.theme Your filter, time range and light/dark choices.
ipotracker.budget, ipotracker.aitarget The application budget you typed, and which AI site you last chose.

Your PAN is the sensitive one, so to be explicit about it: it is stored only to save you retyping it, it is copied to your clipboard when you press a check button, and it is never included in anything sent off the device — not in the AI prompt, not anywhere. The allotment check works by opening the registrar's own site and letting you paste; the app cannot look up an allotment for you, because every registrar puts a CAPTCHA in front of it.

To erase it

Remove individual IDs with the × beside them on My Applications. To remove everything, clear site data for this domain in your browser settings. Nothing survives that — there is no copy anywhere else.

What does leave your browser

  • Loading the site. It is served by GitHub Pages, and may also be served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they see the request — your IP address, browser and the file requested — and keep their own logs under their own policies. The app sets nothing and asks for nothing.
  • Market data. The figures are fetched as plain files from this same site. They are refreshed by a scheduled job that runs away from your browser, so normal use makes no request to NSE, IPO Watch or anyone else.
  • The fallback path. If those files cannot be reached, the app can fall back to fetching from the sources through a public relay (allorigins.win, codetabs.com or corsproxy.io). Those relays see that request. It contains no personal data, but you should know it happens.
  • Ask AI. Pressing an “Ask AI” button opens Google, ChatGPT or Claude with a prepared question. That question contains the IPO figures shown on screen and, if you entered one, your application budget. It never contains your PAN. What happens to it after that is governed by whichever service you opened.
  • Outbound links. Registrar sites, NSE, BSE and prospectus documents are other people's websites with their own policies.

Children

This is a tool for adults dealing with their own investments and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, the change will be visible in the source, which is public and carries the full history. Questions or corrections belong in an issue there.
