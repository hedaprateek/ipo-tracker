Where the figures come from, and how they fail

  • NSE — the issue calendar, price bands, category-wise subscription and corporate actions.
  • IPO Watch — grey market premium, and prospectus summaries such as financials, the timetable and the reservation split.
  • Yahoo Finance — listing-day and current prices.

None of these is an official feed to this site. They are read from public pages and endpoints, which means figures can be wrong, stale, incomplete or missing without warning, and a source can change shape or go down at any time. The app shows when each source last answered so you can judge the age of what you are reading — but it cannot tell you when a source is confidently wrong.

Grey market premium in particular

GMP is an unofficial quote from an unregulated, thinly traded market. It is easy to manipulate and it is not a forecast of the listing price. This site grades its own record — how often the premium called the direction correctly, and by how much it missed — precisely so the number is not mistaken for a prediction. Read that record before leaning on the premium.

Automated readings

Where an apply-or-avoid view appears, it is generated by a language model from the figures on the page. It has not read the prospectus, it can be wrong, and it is not a substitute for doing the work. It is labelled as such wherever it appears.
